DMCA Policy

Last Updated: May 09, 2026

Adult Video Platform (“we,” “our,” or “us”) respects the intellectual property rights of others and expects users of our website to do the same.

This DMCA Policy explains how copyright owners and their authorized representatives can report content that they believe infringes their copyrights and how users may submit counter-notifications when content has been removed in error.

1. Reporting Copyright Infringement

If you believe that any content available on Adult Video Platform infringes a copyright you own or are authorized to represent, you may submit a written DMCA takedown notice to our designated copyright agent using the contact information provided below.

Please include all of the following information in your notice:

  1. Your signature. A physical or electronic signature of the copyright owner or a person authorized to act on their behalf.
  2. Identification of the copyrighted work. A description of the copyrighted work you claim has been infringed. If multiple works are involved, you may provide a representative list.
  3. Identification of the allegedly infringing content. The exact URL(s) of the material you want us to review, along with sufficient information to help us locate it.
  4. Your contact information. Your full name, mailing address, telephone number, and email address, where available.
  5. Good-faith statement. A statement that you have a good-faith belief that the disputed use of the material is not authorized by the copyright owner, its agent, or the law.
  6. Accuracy and authority statement. A statement that the information in your notice is accurate and, under penalty of perjury, that you are the copyright owner or are authorized to act on behalf of the owner of an exclusive right allegedly infringed.

Incomplete notices may delay our ability to investigate your complaint. We may request additional information when necessary to identify the work, locate the content, or verify your authority to submit the notice.

2. Designated DMCA Agent

Please use this contact information specifically for copyright-related complaints. General inquiries should be submitted through our Contact Us page.

3. Review and Removal of Reported Content

Upon receiving a sufficiently complete copyright infringement notice, we will review the complaint and take appropriate action in accordance with applicable law.

Depending on the circumstances, this may include removing the reported material, disabling access to it, requesting additional information, or notifying the user responsible for the material.

Where applicable, we will follow the DMCA notice-and-takedown and counter-notification procedures.

We reserve the right to remove content that violates our policies or applicable law, independently of whether a formal DMCA notice has been submitted.

4. Counter-Notification

If you believe that content you uploaded or made available through our website was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification, you may submit a written counter-notification to our designated DMCA agent.

Your counter-notification should include:

  1. Your physical or electronic signature.
  2. Identification of the material that was removed or disabled and the location where it appeared before removal.
  3. A statement, under penalty of perjury, that you have a good-faith belief that the material was removed or disabled as a result of a mistake or misidentification.
  4. Your full name, mailing address, and telephone number.
  5. A statement consenting to the jurisdiction of the appropriate United States federal district court, as specified under the DMCA, and agreeing to accept service of process from the person who submitted the original notice or their authorized representative.

Please send counter-notifications to the designated DMCA agent listed in Section 2.

Upon receiving a counter-notification that satisfies the applicable legal requirements, we will forward it to the original complaining party and follow the procedures and timelines required by applicable law.

Where the DMCA counter-notification procedure applies, content may be restored between 10 and 14 business days after receipt of a qualifying counter-notification unless the designated agent receives notice that the original complaining party has filed a court action seeking to restrain the allegedly infringing activity.

Submitting a counter-notification is a legal process. Please ensure that all information provided is accurate and that you have a legitimate basis for requesting restoration.

5. Repeat Copyright Infringers

We maintain a policy addressing repeat copyright infringement.

Where appropriate and consistent with applicable law, we may suspend or terminate access to our website for users who repeatedly infringe the copyrights of others.

We also reserve the right to take action against accounts involved in serious or repeated violations of our intellectual property policies.

6. Misrepresentation and Abusive Notices

DMCA notices and counter-notifications must be submitted in good faith and contain accurate information.

Knowingly making material misrepresentations that content is infringing, or that content was removed or disabled by mistake or misidentification, may result in legal liability, including liability for damages, costs, and attorneys’ fees under applicable law.

We reserve the right to disregard or take appropriate action in response to complaints that are fraudulent, abusive, or submitted without a legitimate basis, subject to applicable law.

7. Non-Copyright Content Removal Requests

This DMCA procedure is intended for copyright infringement claims. Other types of complaints may require a different review process.

If you believe that content available on our website contains intimate images or videos published without consent, violates your privacy, involves impersonation, or raises another serious safety concern, please contact us through our Contact Us page and clearly describe the issue.

Please provide the relevant URL and sufficient information for us to locate and assess the reported content.

We may use the information submitted with a copyright notice or counter-notification to investigate the complaint, communicate with the relevant parties, and comply with legal obligations.

Where required or appropriate under the applicable legal process, information contained in a notice or counter-notification may be shared with the person who uploaded or reported the material, or with other relevant parties.

Please do not include unrelated sensitive personal information in your submission.

For additional information about how we collect, use, and protect personal information, please review our Privacy Policy.
